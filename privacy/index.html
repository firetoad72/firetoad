Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Fire Toad app on iPhone and Android

In short: everything you record in Fire Toad is stored on your device. The app has no account and no server. It sends nothing to us or anyone else, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Who we are

Fire Toad is published by Michael Tabet, the developer listed on the App Store ("we", "us"). Fire Toad is an offline record-keeping tool for fire safety inspections and preparing annual fire safety statements.

What the app stores, and where

To do its job, the app stores what you enter, on your device only:

  • building details (name, address, BCA class, owner or owners corporation),
  • fire safety schedules, measures, inspection results, notes and defects,
  • photos and documents you attach,
  • your practitioner and company details (name, company, ABN, accreditation or licence number, phone, email, address, logo).

Some of this is personal information about building owners, contacts and practitioners. You decide what to enter.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your data to us or to any third party, and we cannot see or access it. There is no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Purchases

Fire Toad Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we receive no payment details or personal information from it. The app checks your purchase on your device with Apple's StoreKit, so there is still no account and nothing is sent to us.

Camera, photos and microphone

The app asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to attach a photo, for example of a defect. If you record a video, the microphone is used for that recording. These photos and recordings stay on your device inside the app.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it. Exporting a report, the AFSS document, a CSV logbook or a backup, or copying text, opens your device's share sheet or print dialog. You decide where it goes: email, Files, AirDrop, a printer, and so on. Those services handle the data under their own terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it, so keep your own exports or backups of anything important.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security: its passcode, biometrics and encryption. Keep your device locked and up to date.

Children

Fire Toad is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us through the support page.
