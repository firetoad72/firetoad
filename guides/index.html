Guides for fire safety practitioners

Last reviewed 25 October 2026

Short, practical guides to the paperwork and the standards behind a fire safety inspection in NSW. Each one cites the official sources it relies on, and standards are cited by clause number and summarised, never reproduced.

How to prepare an Annual Fire Safety Statement (AFSS) in NSW

Who is responsible, when it is due, the 3-month assessment window, the accredited practitioner's role, the approved form, lodging with council and Fire and Rescue NSW, supplementary statements, late penalties, and a checklist.

AS 1851 routine service frequencies at a glance

Monthly, six-monthly, yearly and longer intervals under AS 1851-2012 for sprinklers, pumps, hydrants, tanks, fire alarms, hose reels, extinguishers, passive systems and mechanical services, by section and table number.

Critical vs non-critical defects in fire safety inspections

The three AS 1851-2012 classifications in plain language, who must be told and how fast, examples by measure, what to do with each, and how to record defects well.

These guides are general guidance, not legal advice. Check the current legislation, the building's fire safety schedule, your licensed copies of the standards and your local council before relying on them.
