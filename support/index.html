Help with Fire Toad on iPhone and Android

Need help or found a bug? Open a support request. Tell us your device (iPhone or Android) and what happened. Please don't include building owners' or other people's personal details.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings and add your practitioner details (name and accreditation). They appear on reports.
  2. Add a building, then its current fire safety schedule. Pick each measure and its standard of performance from the dropdowns.
  3. Tap Start site inspection and record each measure: tick what you verified, mark it satisfactory, defective or N/A, and add a note.
  4. Tap Complete inspection to see the assessment report, then Print / Save as PDF or Generate AFSS statement.

Putting your company on reports

Open Toolkit → Companies and add your company's name, logo, ABN, licence and contact details. On any report, choose the company from Company on this report above the report. Each report remembers its company, and new reports use the one you picked last. Pick Fire Toad (app default) to go back to the standard header.

Sharing the report and the AFSS

On iPhone, Share PDF on the report makes an A4 PDF and opens the share sheet, so you can email it, save it to Files or AirDrop it. Print / Save as PDF opens the print dialog. Generate AFSS statement creates the official NSW form as a Word document and opens the share sheet.

Fire Toad Pro

Fire Toad is free to download. Recording buildings, schedules and inspections, photos, report history, the Toolkit and backups are always free, and your records are always yours.

Your first 3 reports include everything: Share PDF, Print / Save as PDF, your company branding and each report's AFSS document. Fixing and re-completing a report doesn't cost another one. After that, Fire Toad Pro unlocks them for every report. It's a one-off in-app purchase, not a subscription. Open Toolkit → Fire Toad Pro to buy it.

  • New iPhone? Open Toolkit → Fire Toad Pro and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Bought Fire Toad before version 1.3? Pro is included for you at no extra cost, for good. Toolkit → Fire Toad Pro shows it as active.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backing up

Open Toolkit → Backup & restore and tap Save a backup. Fire Toad saves everything, including photos and companies, in one file. On a new phone, use Restore from a backup and pick that file. The dashboard reminds you if you haven't backed up for 30 days.

Your data

Everything is stored on your device. There's no account or cloud copy, so save a backup regularly (see above). Deleting the app deletes its data. See the privacy policy.

Important

Fire Toad is a record-keeping and reference tool. It does not assess, certify or accredit, and its report is not the statutory form. Always assess against the building's fire safety schedule and your own licensed copies of the standards.
