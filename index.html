Fire safety inspections, done on site.

Fire Toad is a field app for fire safety practitioners. Walk the building, record every essential measure, log defects, then hand over a clean assessment report and prepare the NSW Annual Fire Safety Statement. It all runs on your phone, with no account and no signal needed.

Get Fire Toad on the App Store See what it does

iPhone: free to download. Your first 3 reports include sharing, emailing, printing, your company branding and the AFSS document; after that, those need Fire Toad Pro, as a yearly subscription or a one-off lifetime purchase. Recording inspections is always free. Android: every feature included.

Everything for the inspection, in one place

Built around how an Accredited Practitioner (Fire Safety) actually works a site: schedule in hand, measure by measure.

Buildings & schedules

Each building with its BCA class, owner and current fire safety schedule. Pick each essential measure and its standard of performance from a list, with editions, instead of typing them.

Guided site inspections

Work through every measure with a checklist of what to verify. Mark it satisfactory, defective or N/A, and add your own notes.

Defects

Log critical and non-critical defects against a measure, add photos, and track them until they're rectified.

Assessment report

Finish the inspection and get a full Fire Safety Assessment Report: measures, results, checklists, comments and defects. One tap shares it as a PDF.

NSW AFSS

Fill the official NSW Annual Fire Safety Statement template from the inspection, and keep track of AFSS anniversaries and supplementary statements.

AS 1851 logbook

Record routine service activities per measure and export the logbook as CSV.

Practitioner toolkit

Search the standards, browse every essential measure, classify defects, check AFSS readiness, and test yourself with the quiz. All of it works offline.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything is stored on your device, and one tap saves a backup file you can restore on a new phone.

Your company on every report

Fire Toad isn't just for one business. Add your company's name, logo, ABN, licence and contact details once, and every report carries your branding instead of ours. On iPhone, company branding is part of Fire Toad Pro and is included on your first 3 reports.

  • Add as many companies as you work under
  • Pick the company for each report from a dropdown
  • Each report remembers its company; new ones use your last pick
  • Your logo prints at the top of the report and PDF

A record-keeping tool, not a certifier

Fire Toad helps you capture and present your own assessment. It does not assess, certify or accredit anything, and its reports are not the statutory form. Standards are cited by clause number with plain-language summaries, never reproduced. Always assess against the building's fire safety schedule and your own licensed copies of the standards. Fire Toad is not affiliated with the NSW Government or Standards Australia.
